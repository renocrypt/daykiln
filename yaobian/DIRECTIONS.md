## Three processes

| | KILN | RULE | PLUMB |
|---|---|---|---|
| Process | Fire and cooling | A construction rule | Gravity |
| Tradition | Song ceramics: Ru, Jun, Ge | Islamic geometric ornament and muqarnas | Form-finding: Hooke, Gaudí, Otto, Isler |
| Tagline | *The fire chose the blue. The cooling drew the cracks.* | *The rule drew the wall.* | *Gravity drew the arch.* |

**窑变 Yaobian**, kiln change: what a kiln does to a glaze that the potter sets up but never draws. KILN takes it literally; RULE and PLUMB take it as the thesis: the maker sets conditions and stops.

**One ritual.** **Set:** one gesture sets the conditions. **Let go:** control is taken away and the process runs. **Watch.** **Lean in:** a 4× loupe. **Keep:** the drawing the process made, signed by the fire, the rule, or gravity.

**The process is real.** Fracture, the fire front, and the net are simulated; no result is keyframed. The camera stands in a few places and moves only to show what the visitor caused.

## KILN

The visitor carries a bowl to the slip and holds it under: the glaze builds as the square root of the time, as a slip cast does, and pools where a bowl's glaze pools. The kiln closes and fires on a schedule compressed to seconds; the bowl glows at its blackbody colour, then cools, and the crackle runs live, each crack lit gold as it opens, with its own ping.

- **Real:** the glaze is a Kubelka–Munk layer whose colour comes from its thickness; the crackle is sequential channel cracking of a film in tension, run in workers at the dip's thickness, revealed in the order it formed ([src/kiln/](src/kiln/)).
- **Open:** the kiln as a chamber, the saggar and spurs; the bowl crazing on after the kiln.

## RULE

Islamic patterns are constructed: strapwork crosses each edge of a polygon tiling at a fixed contact angle, and the pattern follows. The visitor turns the angle and the wall redraws to its edges. The hall, the Alhambra's Hall of the Two Sisters, stands in carved crystal; let go, a line of fire runs out from where the rule was set and leaves fired clay behind it. Its muqarnas dome is raised from Jones and Goury's plan (1842): 4,206 pieces in 28 tiers.

- **Real:** every line derives from the rule and the tiling; polygons in contact on the 4.8.8, 6.6.6, and 3.12.12 tilings.
- **Open:** the girih tiles, and with them the moment a tiling snaps into fivefold symmetry.

## PLUMB

A hanging chain carries its weight in tension; turned over, the same curve carries it in compression. The visitor hangs lead on a net of string; it settles; the model is frozen at rest, turned over, grown thirty times, and the visitor stands under the vault in a sand sea, setting the sun. The record is Gaudí's: the photograph turned over, the strings traced on it, and the weights table.

- **Real:** a 15 × 15 net solved by position-based dynamics at 240 Hz; the standing vault is the hanging net's own knots, mirrored ([src/plumb/](src/plumb/)).
- **Open:** supports the visitor sets, as Gaudí's board set his.
