# PLUMB: sources

Claims in PLUMB's public copy, and where each was checked. Opened 2026-09-24.

## Hooke's anagram

- **Claim as used:** in the log once the model is turned over, stamped *Hooke*: "As hangs the flexible line, so, inverted, stands the rigid arch: his anagram of the 1670s, solved only after his death."
- **Checked:** the anagram, *abcccddeeeeefggiiiiiiiillmmmmnnnnnooprrsssttttttuuuuuuuux*, is in the appendix to Hooke's *A Description of Helioscopes, and Some Other Instruments*; its solution, *ut pendet continuum flexile, sic stabit contiguum rigidum inversum*, was given by his executor Richard Waller in *The Posthumous Works of Robert Hooke*, 1705. The English follows Heyman's translation, shortened.
- **Unsettled:** the book's year, given as 1675 by some sources and 1676 by others, probably printing against title page. Copy says "the 1670s" until the title page is read at its holder.
- Sources: [Catenary, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catenary) as a finding aid; [Block, DeJong and Ochsendorf, Nexus Network Journal 8:2, 2006](https://web.mit.edu/masonry/papers/block_dejong_ochs_NNJ.pdf); [Newton Excel Bach on the anagram's letters](https://newtonexcelbach.com/2010/07/14/arches-anagrams-and-plagiarism/); the Linda Hall Library's scan of the page, to open.

## Gaudí's hanging model for the Colònia Güell

- **Claim as used:** "For the church of the Colònia Güell, from 1898, Antoni Gaudí hung a model of strings from a board and loaded it with small bags of lead shot. He photographed it, turned the photographs upside down, and drew the church over them."
- **Checked:** the commission is of 1898, the crypt built 1908 to 1914 and the church never finished. The model, 1:10 and about 4 m tall, hung in a shed on site from a board carrying the plan; strings weighted with sacks of lead shot; Gaudí painted over inverted photographs of it in gouache or pastel. The model did not survive; a reconstruction is shown at the Colònia Güell interpretation centre.
- **Not used, still to verify:** the Stuttgart reconstruction by Frei Otto's institute.
- Sources: [Church of Colònia Güell, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Col%C3%B2nia_G%C3%BCell) as a finding aid; [Gaudí's hanging chain models, List of Physical Visualizations](https://dataphys.org/list/gaudis-hanging-chain-models/). To open at the holder: the Càtedra Gaudí.
