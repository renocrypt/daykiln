# Yaobian: look

Many styles, each piece in its own, every one dazzling and finished up close. [DIRECTIONS.md](DIRECTIONS.md) says what each piece is; this says how it looks and moves.

## Shared

- **The held opening.** Each piece opens on a composed still with its name over it, and holds until the visitor scrolls or begins.
- **Words on the scene.** Never in boxes; over a scrim where the scene is bright; withdrawn when the result is the thing.
- **One label face.** Martian Mono for every label, readout, and caption; hairline leaders one device pixel wide.
- **Finished up close.** Under the 4× loupe a piece is as finished as the whole.
- **No worn-out faces.** No Google Fonts, no face the design-literate web names on sight. Any downloadable face may be the model for drawn lettering.
- **No clichés.** No "sacred geometry"; no dragon, jade, lotus, silk, zen, or qi in copy or ornament.

## Motion and type

The web's current tools, each where it is strongest: WebGPU and TSL for the scenes; Anime.js (`splitText`, `spring`, timelines) for type.

## The pieces

**KILN.** The glaze is the only colour in the room. A strip softbox behind and left draws one long highlight in the bowl; firing, the bowl's blackbody light is the only source, then the cracks open gold and fade to their stain. Macro depth of field; Neutral tone mapping so the blue holds.

**RULE.** A hall carved in crystal: dark, glassy, cold at its edges. Let go, a line of fire runs out from where the rule was set and leaves fired clay behind it, the carving kept. The sun enters through the drum's windows; the rest is what the room returns.

**PLUMB.** A workshop at dusk: one north window, plaster the light, lead and string the structure. Turned over and grown thirty times, the vault stands in a sand sea under Plates' baked sky, the sun set by the visitor from afternoon to dusk; 74° under the vault, sharp.

## Mobile

Portrait. The scene keeps the middle; the steps, the newest note, and the actions keep the edges. PLUMB's camera stands back to keep the model's width; under the vault the lens widens instead.
