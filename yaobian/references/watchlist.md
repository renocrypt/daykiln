# Watch list

The works to look at, and two boundary references per piece for what a frame must not become.

## KILN

| Work | Look at |
|---|---|
| Ru ware narcissus basin, National Palace Museum, Taipei | The one famous Ru piece with no crackle: how the glaze alone carries the object; the rim thinning to the body |
| Ru ware dishes and bowls, Sir Percival David Collection, British Museum | Crackle spacing against thickness; spur marks on the foot; where the glaze pools |
| Jun ware with copper flush | Color that no one placed: the edge of a flush against the blue |
| Jian ware 曜变 bowl, Seikadō Bunko | Iridescent spots in black glaze; the limit case of kiln change |
| Ge ware with 金丝铁线 | Two generations of cracks stained differently; the accident kept as ornament |
| 天工开物, 1637, ceramics chapter plates | The kiln and saggar as woodblock diagrams; the register of a working drawing |
| Boundary: decorative "crackle glaze" tableware; 3D shader-ball porcelain demos | Crackle as a texture tile; glaze as a coat of paint with a highlight |

## RULE

| Work | Look at |
|---|---|
| Hall of the Two Sisters muqarnas dome, Alhambra | Cells in tiers; the light falling through; stucco with no color |
| North dome, Friday Mosque of Isfahan | Brick geometry carrying pattern into structure |
| Darb-i Imam spandrel, Isfahan | A quasi-periodic tiling; the rule at the edge of the field |
| Sheikh Lotfollah dome interior | The field converging on the apex; haft-rangi tile color |
| Topkapı Scroll plates, Necipoğlu 1995 | Construction lines left visible; a working drawing, not a finished ornament |
| Bonner's construction diagrams | The contact angle; how one rule yields several families |
| Boundary: "sacred geometry" posters; pattern-generator app interfaces | Pattern as mysticism; pattern as a tool's output with a toolbar |

## PLUMB

| Work | Look at |
|---|---|
| Gaudí's hanging model for Colònia Güell, and its reconstruction | Strings, weights, the inverted photograph with drawing over it |
| Frei Otto's soap-film photographs, Institute for Lightweight Structures | Minimal surfaces on black; the frame as the only authored element |
| Mannheim Multihalle hanging-chain model, 1975 | A gridshell found by hanging; how the model becomes the building |
| Heinz Isler's shells, for example the Deitingen service station | Hanging cloth frozen into concrete; thinness |
| Hooke's anagram page, 1676 | One sentence as the whole principle |
| Boundary: parametric-architecture renders; cloth-simulation demos | Form authored then justified; physics as spectacle |

## Register calibration, web-native

Openable in a browser; verified reachable on 2026-09-24 in the sister project. Check that WebGL works still run.

- Rafaël Rozendaal, intotime.com: a color field and a gesture; the courage of a screen with nothing on it.
- Studio Moniker, clickclickclick.click: register through refusal; a voice that is light and unsettling.
- AATOAA and NFB, Way to Go: presence, sound, and camera discipline; experience first.
- Tyler Hobbs, Fidenza: field composition and restraint with no interface; each output a print.
- Bartosz Ciechanowski, Mechanical Watch: the third-register ceiling that every Yaobian piece must clear in experience and match in craft.
- Craig Kaplan's Taprats: the pattern tool, and the boundary. RULE is not a tool with a toolbar.
