# Shared: asset research

Researched on 2026-09-23 and 2026-09-24. Catalogue only; nothing downloaded. Publisher preview thumbnails were viewed to judge fit; no asset files were fetched.

> **2026-09-24:** The Role column records license status from this first pass. It does not say what is picked: selection is now beauty-first, and current picks are in [Selection in DIRECTIONS.md](../DIRECTIONS.md#selection).

## Top picks

| Need | Top pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Typefaces | Newsreader (titles) with IBM Plex Mono (labels) | An editorial serif whose optical-size axis runs from caption to display in one file, paired with an engineered monospace that has CJK siblings. Both OFL. |
| 2. HDRI | Poly Haven `monochrome_studio_03`, with a procedural environment in the piece | One large softbox under a dark ceiling: a single broad highlight on brass and bronze and quiet dark reflections. CC0. |
| 3. Materials | Paper: ambientCG Paper006. Brass: ambientCG Metal048B. Bronze: cgbookcase Bronze 02. Plaster: Poly Haven `white_stucco`. Charcoal: Poly Haven `painted_concrete_02`. | All CC0 and at least 4K. The picks favor fine, even surfaces that hold up under macro depth of field. |
| 4. Tooling | KTX2 textures, built with glTF-Transform or KTX-Software and loaded with three.js `KTX2Loader`. ## Gaps

**Not found**

- **Fibrous paper.** No CC0 PBR scan of rag or laid paper was found at 2K or higher. ambientCG's paper set is "Approximation" and reads smooth; 3dtextures.me is CC0 but free only at 1K. The best paper surface may come from UNFOLD's historical gore scans, which are in UNFOLD's scope, or from a procedural fiber normal map made with Canvas, as the reference makes its textures.
- **True brass and verdigris bronze.** ambientCG returned no results for "brass" or "patina". The brass picks are gold materials to be re-tinted. The only patinated option, ambientCG Metal058B, is copper with a green-blue hue that sits outside WAKE's palette.

**Needs a decision**

- **Procedural environment or shipped HDRI.** The reference ships no HDRI. Recommendation: use a procedural environment per direction and use the HDRIs for calibration only. If one ships, budget about 1.3–1.6 MB for a 1K `.hdr`, or less as a gain-map JPEG.
- **WebGL 2 or WebGPU.** N8AO and pmndrs v6 are WebGL only. three.js TSL has depth of field, bloom, GTAO, and motion blur in core, but requires `WebGPURenderer`. WAKE's motion blur and history buffers may tip this decision, so decide before the first prototype.
- **Upgrade coupling.** pmndrs v6 caps three below 0.187, so each three.js upgrade waits on a pmndrs release. Pin both.
- **Type weight.** Newsreader costs 132 KB (roman) plus 147 KB (italic) for the Latin subset alone. Test whether labels and titles need the italic. Trimming the axis ranges with a font instancer was not tested.
- **CJK.** Whether any copy is localized. If it is, budget for sliced delivery, not a single file.

**Caveats**

- Fit judgements come from publisher preview thumbnails. Confirm each pick in a lookdev pass in the real renderer, with the direction's palette, tone mapping, and depth of field.
- cgbookcase does not state file sizes, and its normal maps are DirectX-convention.
- N8AO's license differs between its repository (CC0 1.0) and npm (ISC). Both are permissive; record both.
- Basis Universal 2.50's new formats (XUASTC, XUBC7) are not referenced in three.js r186 `KTX2Loader`. KTX-Software 5 is still a release candidate.
- Not surveyed: Fontshare (ITF Free Font License) and the OFL foundries Velvetyne and Collletttivo; Poliigon and Fab/Quixel, which are not CC0; and the Google Fonts `popularity` field's exact definition.
