# WAKE: asset research

Researched on 2026-09-23 (completed 2026-09-24). Catalogue only; nothing downloaded.

> **2026-09-24:** The Role column records license status from this first pass. It does not say what is picked: selection is now beauty-first, and current picks are in [Selection in DIRECTIONS.md](../DIRECTIONS.md#selection).

Roles follow [DIRECTIONS.md](../DIRECTIONS.md): **in the piece** means the stated license permits shipping in a public web experience; **reference only** means it informs design and is never shipped. Licenses are quoted as stated on the page opened. "Not stated" means the page opened did not say.

## Top picks

1. **Wake studies:** Johansson et al. 2018 flycatcher wakes, with the [Dryad dataset](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.745q5) (public domain). One study measures 3D wakes from 1 to 9 m/s, so the change from slow to fast flight appears as a single sequence; the data can check or seed a recorded-wake fallback.
2. **Kinematics:** Liu et al.'s two-jointed seagull wing (three angles as Fourier series), available openly through the [CC BY 2022 reproduction](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/11/5440), scaled to Pennycuick's herring gull: 3.13 Hz at 11.8 m/s. A procedural stroke with real gull numbers.
3. **Marey:** the 1901 smoke photographs from the 57-channel machine (Cinémathèque française). White threads bending around an obstacle on black are WAKE's palette and subject in one image. Rights need a decision; the clean shippable fallback is the Met's [*[Bird in Flight]*](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/286544) (public domain).
4. **Boccioni:** the Met's bronze, [DT6411 and DT6413](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Unique_Forms_of_Continuity_in_Space_MET_DT6411.jpg) (CC0). Even museum light on dark bronze; about 3000 × 4000 px.
5. **Bird model:** no shippable close-up gull exists. Build it procedurally from the Blackburn Lab [common gull wing skeleton](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/common-gull-wing-skeleton-3215f6a2a0a14624838f4a23b41f081c) (reference) and Harvey's gull wing landmarks (CC BY), and render the frozen poses as a bronze cast after Marey's 1887 sculpture.
6. **Rendering:** Weißmann and Pinkall 2010, [filament-based smoke with vortex shedding](https://page.math.tu-berlin.de/~pinkall/forDownload/filaments.pdf): shed vortex filaments plus advected smoke, designed for real time.

## Gaps

- **No gull wake.** No measured wake of a gull or of any bird that large was found. The form must be inferred from passerines and swifts (measured) and a bald ibis (simulated). Label the wake as a model.
- **No open flapping-gull kinematics across speeds.** The practical path is Liu's seagull model (one flight condition), scaled with Pennycuick's field frequencies. Speed-dependent changes (amplitude, downstroke fraction, upstroke folding) come from pigeon and passerine studies and must be labeled as borrowed.
- **The Beaune bronze.** No online record, open photograph, or 3D scan was found. Contact the Beaune museum for images, terms, and whether a scan exists. The number of figures in the Beaune bronze was not confirmed from a primary record.
- **Rights decision on Marey reproductions.** The Cinémathèque française captions the 1901 smoke plates "© Cinémathèque française". The Collège de France requires authorization for public or commercial use. The works themselves are Marey's (d. 1904). Decide whether to request high-resolution scans and permission (the cleanest route, and the only route to macro-quality files) or rely on public-domain status with the small Commons copies.
- **Is Chroma commercial?** This decides whether the Collège de France's free non-commercial reuse covers the piece.
- **Resolution.** The Commons smoke photographs are about 1600 px tall: too small for macro framing.
- **Web budget.** The Dryad wake data is 17.28 GB and the UCLouvain simulation is 103 files of about 1.7 GB each. Either must be reduced offline (for example to vortex-core polylines or a few slices) before shipping.
- **Paywalled key papers.** Spedding et al. 2003, Tobalske and Dial 1996, and Liu et al. 2006 were read only as abstracts or through secondary sources.
- **Unverified identity.** The 2014 Commons photographs of a 57-channel smoke machine do not state whether the object is an original or a 2004 reconstruction.
